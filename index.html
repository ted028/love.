DEAR PITSO LAWRENCE KAISARA

A Letter Written With Love

Some feelings are too beautiful to leave unspoken. So I put mine into words.

Dear Lawrence,

From last year until now, my life has been a mess, and after last year’s heartbreak I honestly did not think I would ever date again, or love someone fully. I was so afraid of being heartbroken again. It was not only about the heartbreak, but also about being loved right.

I’ve been afraid of opening up ever since, which is why I was so skeptical about starting to date. I was not sure I was ready to love again. I had always felt unappreciated, and it left me unable to feel the joy of dating.

“And then quite unexpectedly, there you were”

You made me reconsider whether I was simply afraid, or had just loved the wrong person. We had only talked about how we both know I will drink sooner or later, but I already found my feelings growing towards you.

We started talking, and 07/11/2026 was the day it all began. It felt right from our first meetup. A warmth I cannot explain, and a certain kind of peace I feel every time I am around you. Not forced, not complicated. Just right.

I love how alike we are, and sometimes I wish you were here so I could spend the whole night just admiring you. Your presence makes me so happy, which is why I am always smiling around you. Seeing you go from nonchalant to bubbly, lively and emotional around me made me see how much you really love us. You are incredibly funny.

“O ska lela tloga ke go lata ka mophane”

I have so much to tell, but words alone could never explain how much I love you. You made me feel something I have never felt before. I want to spend more time with you and be your pillow of comfort. I look forward to the ice cream dates, exploring restaurants, cooking together, and even the little disagreements we will laugh about in the future.

I want to love you right and bring peace to your life like you do to mine. I want you to feel appreciated, adored and considered. I am not promising to do it perfectly, because I am only human, but I will do it to the best of my ability.

Let me spare you the long paragraphs. All in all, I guess what I want to say is…

Will you make me the happiest man and officially be MY boy/babe?

I’d love for you to be mine. Not to own you, but to enjoy every moment of us being together.

YOU JUST MADE ME THE HAPPIEST.
